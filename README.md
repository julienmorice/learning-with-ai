# Learning with AI — PracTice Welcome Workshop

Presentation of the AI block of the welcome workshop for new students
at IMT Business School, 31 August 2026.

**Online: https://julienmorice.github.io/learning-with-ai/**

Julien Morice — PracTice team, IMT Business School.

English adaptation of [apprendre-avec-ia](https://github.com/julienmorice/apprendre-avec-ia).

## Flow

1. **`gen-z.html`** — opening page, "Gen Z in the Age of AI", with the
   context-setting video and a button leading into the presentation.
2. **`index.html`** — the presentation, 22 slides.

## Usage

Self-contained HTML page, 22 slides, scaled 16:9 scene.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `→` `space` `Enter` | next slide |
| `←` `Backspace` | previous slide |
| `F` | fullscreen |
| `S` | clickable contents (or click the slide number) |
| `Esc` | close the contents |
| `Home` / `End` | first / last slide |

The address keeps the position (`#12`), so you can pick up where you
left off after a reload.

### On phone and tablet

Without a mouse there is no hover, and without a keyboard no shortcuts:
the controls are therefore shown permanently on touch devices.

- **Swipe** left or right to change slide.
- The **arrows** ‹ › are visible at the bottom (portrait) or on the sides (landscape).
- The **grid button** at the bottom left opens the contents.
- Over a video player, the swipe belongs to the player: use the arrows.

In portrait, the 16:9 scene shrinks to a narrow band and the text becomes
small; a hint invites you to rotate the device. **The presentation reads in
landscape.**

## Videos

The five demonstrations are IP Paris MediaServer (UbiCast) players embedded
as `iframe`s. Each video slide also carries an "Open the video in a new tab"
link, useful if the player fails to load.
